Two things before you pick.
1 · Neither font has one Cyrillic glyph. Bosieh 95 glyphs, Kabisat 151 — Latin and digits only, nothing in U+0400–U+04FF. They can only ever touch the 101, Latin words and numerals; every Russian line stays on Golos. I gave both a Latin-only unicode-range so Cyrillic can never silently fall into them.
3 · Kabisat cannot render the 101 at all — checked with fontTools, its ten digit glyphs are empty outlines. It is only ever an option for Latin words, e.g. an accent line, never a numeral.
2 · Bosieh ships under 1001Fonts “Free For Personal Use”. The EULA in the zip names “websites for companies” as commercial and requires written permission from the author. Solutions101 is commercial. Kabisat is a “Demo” cut, normally the same restriction. Converting to woff2 is explicitly allowed, so the files are fine — the usage is the open question, and it is your call.
